FlipWordy

Build a stronger English vocabulary, one word at a time.

FlipWordy is an English vocabulary learning app designed to make daily word practice feel simple, focused, and sustainable. Track your progress, save the words you want to revisit, and build a habit with reminders and home screen widgets.

What FlipWordy offers

  • Daily English word learning with progress tracking.
  • Saved words to help you revisit the vocabulary that matters most.
  • Reminder notifications to support a regular study habit.
  • Home screen widgets for quick access to words during the day.
  • Adjustable preferences such as language and difficulty-related learning settings.
  • An optional subscription to remove ads.

About the app

FlipWordy focuses on clarity over clutter. Instead of overwhelming you with a complicated course structure, it helps you stay in touch with English vocabulary every day through short, repeatable interactions. The goal is to make word learning easier to return to, easier to maintain, and easier to fit into real life.
