<p data-i18n="privacy.intro">Focusling is built to work without collecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what stays on your device, what the app stores, and which platform services are involved.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section1">1. Who we are</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.who">Focusling is a pomodoro focus timer developed by Merve Pekyürek.</p>
<p data-i18n-html="privacy.contact">If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: <a href="mailto:focuslingapp@gmail.com">focuslingapp@gmail.com</a>.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section2">2. Data we collect</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.none">We do not collect any personal data. Focusling has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services. We cannot see how you use the app.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section3">3. Data stored on your device</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.localIntro">To work, Focusling stores the following only on your device:</p>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.local1">Your focus sessions (start time, length and whether they were finished), which are used for your statistics and your ling's growth.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.local2">Your tasks and their pomodoro estimates.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.local3">Your settings, such as timer lengths, strict mode and app blocking.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.local4">Your ling's wardrobe: the items you bought with seeds and the ones it wears.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.local5">Whether Focusling Pro is active, so the app knows it right away when it starts.</li>
</ul>

<p data-i18n="privacy.localBody">This data is removed when you delete the app. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) depending on your device settings.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section4">4. Permissions</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.perm1">Notifications: to show the live countdown and to tell you when a session ends.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.perm2">Exact alarms (Android): so the end-of-session alert arrives on time.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.perm3">App blocking (optional, Pro): Screen Time on iPhone, and usage access plus "display over other apps" on Android. These are used only on your device to keep the apps you picked closed while you focus. The list of apps you pick is never sent anywhere.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.perm4">Widgets and the Live Activity read the timer state from a storage area shared only with Focusling's own widgets.</li>
</ul>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.sectionHealth">5. Apple Health and Health Connect</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.health1">If you turn on "Save to Apple Health" or "Save to Health Connect" in Settings, Focusling writes each focus session you complete as a mindfulness session (its start and end time) to Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. It is off by default and asks for your permission first.</p>
<ul>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.health2">Focusling only writes mindful minutes. It does not read any health or fitness data.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.health3">This data goes from Focusling to the health app on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising, sold or shared.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.health4">Focusling's use of information from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from HealthKit is handled following Apple's HealthKit guidelines.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.health5">You can turn this off in Focusling's Settings, take back the permission in the Health app (Sharing → Apps) or in Health Connect, and delete the saved minutes there.</li>
</ul>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section5">6. Purchases</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.purchases1">Focusling Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Payment, billing and your purchase history are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment details.</p>
<p data-i18n="privacy.purchases2">The app checks with the store on your device whether Pro is active. Google Play's billing services may process purchase and device information as described in Google's privacy policy.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section6">7. Children</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.children">Focusling does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. In-app purchases are subject to the parental controls of the App Store and Google Play.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section7">8. Your choices</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.choice1">You can turn off notifications, app blocking and saving to Apple Health or Health Connect in the app or in your device settings.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.choice2">You can manage or cancel a Pro subscription in the App Store or Google Play.</li>
  <li data-i18n="privacy.choice3">You can delete all your Focusling data by deleting the app.</li>
</ul>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.section8">9. Changes to this policy</h2>

<p data-i18n="privacy.changes">If Focusling's handling of data changes, we will update this page with a new effective date.</p>
