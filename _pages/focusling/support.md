<p data-i18n="support.intro">For questions about the timer, your ling, Focusling Pro or privacy, contact the support address below.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="support.contactTitle">Contact</h2>

<p><span data-i18n="support.emailLabel">Email:</span> <a href="mailto:focuslingapp@gmail.com">focuslingapp@gmail.com</a></p>
<p data-i18n="support.responseTarget">We usually reply within 3 to 5 business days.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="support.includeTitle">What to include in your message</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n="support.include1">Your app version (Settings, at the bottom) and device model.</li>
  <li data-i18n="support.include2">A short description of the issue.</li>
  <li data-i18n="support.include3">Screenshots if the problem is visual or about a purchase.</li>
</ul>

<h2 data-i18n="support.commonTitle">Common topics</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common1"><strong>Focusling Pro:</strong> If you reinstalled the app or changed phones, open the Pro screen and tap <strong>Restore purchases</strong>. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Cancellations and refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common2"><strong>Late or missing alerts:</strong> Make sure notifications are allowed for Focusling. On Android, also allow alarms &amp; reminders and turn off battery optimization for the app.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common3"><strong>App blocking:</strong> On iPhone, allow Screen Time access when asked. On Android, allow usage access and "display over other apps" from the app's blocking settings.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common4"><strong>Widgets:</strong> After an update, a widget may need to be removed and added again.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common5"><strong>Your data:</strong> Focusling has no account, so your sessions live only on this device. Deleting the app deletes them.</li>
</ul>
