<p><strong data-i18n="privacy.updatedLabel">Last updated:</strong> <span data-i18n="privacy.updatedDate">November 4, 2026</span></p>

<p data-i18n="privacy.intro">Dracora ("the app") is a logic puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.summaryTitle">Summary</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.summary">Dracora does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It does not contain ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not need an account or an internet connection.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.deviceTitle">What stays on your device</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.device">To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your own device: your level progress and scales, settings, achievements, daily puzzle results, your nest of dragons and unfinished boards. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.backupsTitle">Backups</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.backups">Your operating system may include the app's saved data in its own device backup or device transfer (for example Android backup or iCloud backup), under your own account and settings. We do not receive or have access to these backups.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.sharingTitle">Sharing</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.sharing">If you choose to share a result, the app hands an image or text to your system's share sheet. You decide whether and where it goes. The app does not send anything on its own.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.permissionsTitle">Permissions</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.permissions">The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it has no internet permission on Android.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.childrenTitle">Children</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.children">The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.deletingTitle">Deleting your data</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.deleting">Open Settings and choose "Reset progress" to clear progress, daily results, nest, badges and unfinished boards, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="privacy.changesTitle">Changes</h2>
<p data-i18n="privacy.changes">If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.</p>
