<div class="fw-content-card">
  <h4 data-i18n="offers.title">What Dracora offers</h4>
  <ul>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item1">Six chapters, each with its own dragon and a fresh twist: rocks that block cells, sparks that leap like a knight, wind that carries flames around the board edges, and twin dragons that need two flames per row, column and region.</li>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item2">Each level earns one to three scales, based on how cleanly you solved it.</li>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item3">Puzzles graded by the techniques they need, so difficulty grows in a way you can feel.</li>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item4">A new puzzle every day. Solve it to keep your nest streak warm; a free nest blanket can protect your streak once in a while.</li>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item5">Your nest: collect eggs as you play and fill your nest with friendly, calm dragons at your own pace.</li>
    <li data-i18n="offers.item6">Light and dark looks, gentle haptics and a reduce motion option.</li>
  </ul>
