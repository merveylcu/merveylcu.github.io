<h2 data-i18n="support.commonTitle">Common topics</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common1"><strong>Your data:</strong> Dracora has no account, so your progress lives only on your device. Open Settings and choose "Reset progress" to clear it, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common2"><strong>Backups:</strong> Your operating system may include the app's saved data in its own backup or device transfer (for example Android backup or iCloud backup), under your own account and settings.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common3"><strong>Offline:</strong> The app does not need an internet connection.</li>
  <li data-i18n-html="support.common4"><strong>Sharing:</strong> When you share a result, the app hands an image or text to your system's share sheet. You decide where it goes.</li>
</ul>
